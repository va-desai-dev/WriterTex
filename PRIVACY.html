1. Introduction

WriterTex is a macOS rich text word processor and academic project workspace distributed through the Apple App Store (the "App"). The App combines a WYSIWYG tab for descriptive and inferential data analysis, tools to write and export manuscripts, an in-app reference manager with an accompanying Safari extension, and "cite while you write" citation functionality. WriterTex ("WriterTex," "we," "us," or "our") is based in Indiana, United States, and serves customers internationally.
We recognize that manuscripts, research data, and reference libraries are among the most valuable work a person produces. We therefore endeavor to collect only that information reasonably necessary to operate and support the App, and to describe our practices plainly.

1.1 Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") governs personal information that we collect through the App, the Safari extension, and our communications with you (together, the "Services"). It does not govern the practices of third parties, including Apple Inc., whose services you may use in connection with the App and whose own privacy policies shall apply to their processing.
Content that you create and that remains solely on your own device is not "collected" by us unless and until you transmit it to us, for example by attaching it to a support request.

1.2 Applicable Laws

This Policy is intended to satisfy the notice requirements of the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, "CCPA/CPRA"), and of comparable comprehensive state privacy laws, including those of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Indiana, to the extent each applies to us. Where we reference a "legal basis" for processing, we do so for transparency toward all users, including those outside the United States.

1.3 Contacting Us

Questions regarding this Policy or our privacy practices may be directed to vd@vadesai.com. Or you can visit the aforementioned support page at https://vdesai.xyz.

2. What We Collect & Why

The Application does not actively or intentionally collect personal information from you, and it does not transmit your personal information, usage data, or project content to us. We receive only the personal information you voluntarily send us, for example, when you contact us for support. Because we serve users in multiple jurisdictions and are subject to U.S. state privacy laws, we identify below the purpose and legal basis for that limited information. By "legal basis," we mean the justification for processing, whether contract, consent, legitimate interest, or legal obligation.

  1. Information You Provide To Us Directly: Support and correspondence. When you contact us, we receive your email address, the content of your message, and any information you elect to include, so that we may respond and resolve your inquiry. Please do not include sensitive information, such as credentials or private project content, in your message. Legal basis: legitimate interest; consent. We do not otherwise ask you for personal information in order to use the Application.
  2. Information Collected Automatically: The Application does not automatically collect or transmit technical, usage, device, or diagnostic information to us. It does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services that send data to us. Information about how you use the Application remains on your device.
  3. Your Stored Content: The Application is designed so that your project content (source code, documents, notebooks, and similar material) is stored on your device, in AppStorage and Swift data models. It is not transmitted to us in the ordinary operation of the Application. Should you voluntarily transmit content to us, for example in a support request, we shall treat it in accordance with Section 17.

3. How We Use Your Information

Because the Application does not collect personal information, our use of personal information is limited to what you send us. We use it only for the purposes described below, and we do not process it in a manner incompatible with those purposes without providing notice and, where required, obtaining your consent:

  1. Support and communications (legitimate Interest with Consent): To read and respond to messages you send us, provide technical support, and resolve your inquiries.
  2. Legal Compliance (Legal Obligation): Under applicable laws and regulations, we must comply with legal and regulatory requirements and respond to lawful requests warranting a release of information.

4. When We Share Your Information

We never sell your personal data. We do not "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended (the "CCPA"), and comparable laws. We do not routinely disclose personal information to third parties. We disclose it only in the circumstances below:

  1. Legal requirements: We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, subpoena, court order, or other lawful process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of WriterTex, our users, or others.
  2. Business transitions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our assets, personal information may be transferred to the successor entity. Notwithstanding the foregoing, any such successor shall remain bound by the commitments of this Policy as to previously collected information unless you are given notice and, where required, an opportunity to object.
  3. With your consent or at your direction: We shall disclose personal information for any other purpose only with your consent or at your direction.

Depending upon your state of residence, you may hold rights under the CCPA, the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, the Colorado Privacy Act, the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, the Indiana Consumer Data Protection Act, and other state privacy laws. As a matter of policy, we extend the rights below to all users, wherever located, subject to applicable law:

  1. Access: You may request confirmation of whether we process your personal information and obtain a copy of it.
  2. Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  3. Deletion: You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions, such as where retention is required by law or to defend legal claims.
  4. Portability: You may request your information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  5. Opt out of marketing, sale, sharing, and targeted advertising: We do not engage in the sale or sharing of personal information or in targeted advertising, and we do not currently send marketing. You may still submit an opt-out request at any time.
  6. Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal shall not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  7. Restriction and objection: Where applicable, you may request that we restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  8. Non-discrimination: We shall not discriminate against you for exercising any of your rights.

7. Data Retention

The Application does not collect personal information, so there is no Application-generated data for us to retain. Project content and settings remain on your device, and you control them. The only personal information we hold is what you send us directly. We retain it only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was received, and thereafter only as required by law.

  1. Support correspondence: Retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and resolve your inquiry and to handle any follow-up, and then deleted upon your request or when no longer needed.
  2. Legal holds and disputes: We may retain information beyond the foregoing period where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, or to comply with law.
    The rationale is simple. We keep information only while it is needed to help you, and we retain it beyond that point only where the law obliges us to do so.

8. Cookies & Tracking

The App is a native macOS application and does not depend on browser cookies to function. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for advertising or cross-context behavioral tracking. To the extent any website or web property we operate employs cookies or similar technologies, they shall be limited to those strictly necessary for operation and security, unless you consent otherwise. You may manage cookies through your browser settings, and blocking strictly necessary technologies may impair some functionality. The Safari extension operates within Safari and is subject to the permissions you grant it in your Safari settings. You may disable or remove the extension at any time through Safari's preferences. Because we do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, we do not currently maintain a separate cookie policy. If that changes, we shall update this Policy accordingly.
